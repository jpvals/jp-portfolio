HostNote

Airbnb host dashboard · Personal project

A dashboard I built with AI assistance (Claude) to streamline the day-to-day operations of my Airbnb. Each booking becomes a checklist of guest messages, already filled in with the guest's name, dates, rent, and door code.

HTMLCSSJavaScriptCloudflare PagesCloudflare AccessGoogle Apps ScriptGoogle Drive
  • Guests sign the rental agreement through a short link, uploading a photo ID and drawing or uploading their signature
  • Files everything under the booking and drafts the guest registration email to the building admin, with IDs attached
  • Private by design: login-protected, links reveal nothing once used, and guest data is permanently deleted 14 days after check-out unless there's an open damage claim
  • I defined the features, workflow, and privacy rules, tested it on my real bookings, and deployed it, using AI to write and refine the code
